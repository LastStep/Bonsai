## What is different now

`bonsai now` prints the time to write in a file: the UTC time to the minute by default, `--day` the UTC day, `--iso`
the millisecond instant, `--local` the person's own time with its offset, `--json` all of them (`bonsai.now/1`).
`internal/timefmt` is the one package for every form (record forms UTC, display converted), with parsers that still
read the legacy zone-less forms. The days Bonsai itself writes (new STATE's `updated`, the personal memory index, the
run-report retention cutoff) are UTC days now. `base:operating-bonsai` tells agents to take every time from `bonsai now`.
