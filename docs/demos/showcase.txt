# docs/demos/showcase.txt: the story edit.sh cuts from the clip set, read section by section. One section per output:
# [showcase] (showcase.mp4), [first-project] (first-project.gif), [session] (session.gif). Lines:
#
#   card <seconds> | <name> | <line under it> | <second line>      the title card (the showcase only)
#   chapter <seconds> | <number> | <title>                         a chapter card
#   end <seconds> | <name> | <address> | <line under it>           the end card
#   scene <clip> <layout> <in> <out> <speed> | <chapter label>     one clip: its name as film.sh writes it, the layout
#        (whole: the clip's whole frame, 1100 x 900 or 1600 x 900; claude: Claude Code's pane, and in the showcase the picture with its lit step
#        over the live view beside it), the in and out points in the clip's own seconds (out may be auto: the last
#        change plus TAIL), a playback speed (1 is as filmed), and the label the caption bar shows at its right
#   gif <width> <colours> <fps>                                    a GIF section's width in pixels, its colours and
#        its frames a second (default 830 32 10: under 1 MB for 40 s of a 160-column terminal; GitHub shows a README
#        image at about 830 px wide, and the README's limits are 1 MB for the first and 5 MB for the second)
#   cap <from> <to> <lit> | <caption>                              a caption of the scene above, in the clip's own
#        seconds (to may be end); lit names the step the picture lights while it shows (agent, guard, allowed,
#        refused, log, ladder, green, red) or - for none. One line of plain words, under about 90 characters in the
#        showcase and 70 in a GIF.
#
# The times are those of the practice clips of 22 Oct 2026 (a take differs: Claude's replies are its own). For a new
# take, read the new times off work/stills/<clip>.png and work/<clip>.big (the header of edit.sh says how). The idle
# stretches between changes are cut by the script, so only the moments need placing.
